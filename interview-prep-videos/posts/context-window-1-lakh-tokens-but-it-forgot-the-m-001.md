EP 14 · It fit in the context window. The AI still missed page 100. Why?

A 200-page policy (about 1 lakh tokens) went into a 128K window. Pages 1 and 200 were answered right; page 100 was confidently wrong. A big window is capacity, not guaranteed recall. The 2023 'Lost in the Middle' study saw a U-shaped pattern: information at the start and end was used best, the middle worst. Newer models do better on simple needle tests, but benchmarks like RULER still show drops as context grows.

And chat 'memory'? The model is stateless. The app resends your history every turn, ChatGPT's Memory notes live outside the model, and when the window fills, old messages get dropped or summarized.
