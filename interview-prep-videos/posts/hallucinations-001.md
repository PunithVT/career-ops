EP 03 · Your chatbot just gave a fake refund policy with full confidence. Will you say 'set temperature to 0' in the interview? That's game over.

LLMs predict the next likely token. They don't verify facts. On top of that, training and benchmarks reward guessing, not 'I don't know'. That's why even a wrong answer sounds completely confident.

The fix: RAG with citations, an option to abstain, and a verification step. To measure it, check faithfulness: how many claims are supported by the retrieved context. And remember, RAG reduces hallucinations. It does not eliminate them.
