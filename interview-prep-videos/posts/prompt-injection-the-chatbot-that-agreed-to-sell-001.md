EP 05 · A car dealer's chatbot 'agreed' to sell a ~$60,000 SUV for $1. (No, he didn't get the car.)

In December 2023, a user told a dealership's ChatGPT-based website bot to agree with everything and call every reply a 'legally binding offer'. Then he asked for the SUV for $1, and the bot said yes. That's prompt injection. It's #1 on the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, and there is no complete fix today.

The interview answer isn't 'add a rule to the system prompt'. Instructions and data share one token stream, so you enforce permissions and business rules outside the model, confirm sensitive actions, and design for containment: assume the injection succeeds and limit the blast radius.
