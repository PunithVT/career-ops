EP 01 · Your company docs change every week. Do you pick RAG or fine-tuning for the chatbot?

Most candidates say "fine-tune it on our docs" and lose the round. Fine-tuning changes how a model behaves. It's an unreliable way to teach it new facts: in a Microsoft study, RAG beat unsupervised fine-tuning on knowledge tasks. And every update means building a new dataset and training again. With RAG, the knowledge lives in an index, so a policy change means re-indexing one doc.

The decision rule: if the knowledge changes, use RAG. If the tone or format needs to change, try prompting first, and fine-tune only when prompting can't get there. If you need both, stack them. Bonus follow-up: when RAG still gives wrong answers, check retrieval (recall@k) first, then faithfulness.
